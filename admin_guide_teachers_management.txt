# Admin Guide - Teacher Management

## How to Add Teachers

### Option 1: Bulk Import (Recommended for multiple teachers)

1. **Prepare your CSV file:**
   - Download template: [teacher_template.csv]
   - Fill in teacher information
   - **Leave Photo column EMPTY** (photos will be auto-generated)

2. **Import:**
   - Click "Import Teachers" button
   - Select your CSV file
   - Wait for success message
   - ✅ Done! Teachers appear with auto-generated avatars

3. **Add Photos (Optional):**
   - Hover over teacher card
   - Click camera icon 📷
   - Select photo from computer
   - ✅ Photo uploaded!

### Option 2: Manual Entry (For single teacher)

1. Click "Add Teacher" button
2. Fill the form with teacher details
3. Click "Add Teacher"
4. Hover over new card → Click 📷 → Upload photo

## Photo Guidelines

- **File Format:** JPG, PNG, GIF
- **Size:** Under 2MB
- **Dimensions:** Square photos work best (100x100px or larger)
- **If no photo:** System auto-generates avatar with initials

## Troubleshooting

**Q: Teacher shows "undefined"**
A: Make sure CSV has "First Name" and "Last Name" columns filled

**Q: Photo not showing**
A: Leave Photo column empty in CSV, upload manually after import

**Q: Can't delete teacher**
A: You must be logged in as admin
